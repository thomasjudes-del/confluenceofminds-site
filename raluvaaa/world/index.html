A LIVING MAP OF HUMAN INTENTIONS

Make a wish. Watch it become alive.

In RALUVAAA, a wish is not a post. It is a seed. It grows, branches, receives help, connects to other trajectories and can eventually bloom.

Enter the living world ↗ See how it works ↓
Prototype in progress

What if the internet showed what we are trying to become, rather than only who we already are?

01 · MAKE A WISH

It starts with a seed.

You release an intention. It becomes an independent lineage, visible in a shared world. No popularity score, no profile to optimize: just a direction beginning to take shape.

A wish is born
02 · BREAK IT DOWN

Split the dream into smaller actions.

An intention can pivot, divide into more concrete steps, then branch again. Earlier paths remain visible: the world keeps the memory of what you tried.

A lineage branches
03 · GET SUPPORT

Support becomes visible.

Other humans can encourage, offer concrete help or suggest a branch. Their support can nourish the trajectory without taking control of your wish.

A branch receives support
04 · CONNECT

Trajectories can meet.

Two lineages can connect when the people involved want them to. The connection becomes a bridge, not a forced merge.

Two lineages connect
05 · BLOOM

When one branch reaches the end, the whole tree can bloom.

Completing a branch triggers a cascading bloom: the travelled path lights up, then the other branches flower in turn. The wish remains in the world as a living trace.

A cascading bloom

This is not a social network to watch. It is a collective organism to feed.
