THE PROTOTYPE IS STILL EVOLVING

What if the internet mapped what people are trying to become, rather than who they already are?

01 · RELEASE

Make a wish.

A root wish creates its own lineage. It is not a profile, not a post, and not a popularity score. It is the beginning of a visible intention.

02 · EVOLVE

Let it change.

A wish can pivot, split into smaller branches, bloom when accomplished, or be let go. Earlier paths remain visible as history instead of being erased.

03 · OTHERS

Let humans matter.

Other people can encourage, offer concrete help, suggest a branch or propose a connection. Actions that affect another lineage are built around consent, not silent editing.

04 · WORLD

See the collective organism.

The result is not a feed. It is a growing field of intentions, traces and connections, drawn with an organic visual language that changes as the underlying human stories change.

The world only becomes interesting if people carry something into it.
