# RALUVAAA - Private alpha communication draft

## Core promise

RALUVAAA is a living map of human intentions.

Release something you want to make real. Let it evolve. Break it into smaller branches. Help somebody else's wish grow. See what blooms.

**Nothing grows alone.**

## Alpha objective

The alpha is not a feature launch. It is a test of three behaviours:

1. does a new visitor understand the object quickly enough to release a real wish;
2. does the wisher come back to evolve, split, bloom or abandon part of that wish;
3. does seeing other human intentions create a real impulse to encourage, help or connect.

## First invitation copy

### Short version

**What do you want to make real?**

I'm testing RALUVAAA, a living map of human intentions.

Release one real wish. Explore a few wishes from people you don't know. If one moves you, encourage it or offer something useful.

Nothing grows alone.

### French short version

**Qu'est-ce que tu voudrais rendre réel ?**

Je teste RALUVAAA, une carte vivante des intentions humaines.

Dépose un vrai wish. Explore quelques intentions d'inconnus. Si l'une d'elles te parle, encourage-la ou propose quelque chose d'utile.

Nothing grows alone.

## Suggested first waves

- Wave 0: 5 to 10 people who can give direct feedback while the product is still visibly alpha.
- Wave 1: 15 to 25 people who were not involved in the design and receive almost no explanation.
- Wave 2: 30 to 50 people recruited around contexts where people already have intentions they care about.

Do not optimize for sign-ups. Look for real wishes, repeat visits, help proposals, accepted contributions, and spontaneous sharing.

## Minimum alpha metrics

- visit -> wish created;
- wish created -> identity claimed when shared persistence is enabled;
- users who return to their wish;
- evolve / split / bloom / abandon events;
- entrusted wish opened;
- entrusted wish acted on;
- encourage;
- help proposed and accepted;
- connection proposed and accepted;
- shares and visitors from shares;
- qualitative answer to: "Did this make you want to do something about your wish?"

## Communication principle

Lead with the living world and the human intention, not with the mechanics. Avoid presenting RALUVAAA as a task manager, social network, AI coach, productivity app or matching platform.
