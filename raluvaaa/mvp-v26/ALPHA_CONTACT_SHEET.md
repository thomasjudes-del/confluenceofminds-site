# RALUVAAA V26 - Alpha outreach operating sheet

## Invitation

### FR

**Qu'est-ce que tu voudrais rendre réel ?**

Je teste RALUVAAA, une carte vivante des intentions humaines.

Dépose un vrai wish. Explore quelques intentions d'inconnus. Si l'une d'elles te parle, encourage-la ou propose quelque chose d'utile.

**Nothing grows alone.**

### EN

**What do you want to make real?**

I'm testing RALUVAAA, a living map of human intentions.

Release one real wish. Explore a few intentions from people you don't know. If one moves you, encourage it or offer something useful.

**Nothing grows alone.**

## What not to explain before the test

Do not lead with the event grammar, AI roadmap, MCP, renderer, gamification, or product architecture. The first test is whether the object explains enough of itself.

## Questions after use

1. What did you think RALUVAAA was for before I explained anything?
2. Did you release a wish you actually care about? If not, why not?
3. What did you expect to happen next to your wish?
4. Did another person's wish make you want to act?
5. Was anything confusing enough that you stopped?
6. Would you come back tomorrow or in a few days? What for?
7. What would make you share a wish with somebody else?

## Observation notes

Record behaviour before opinions: first click, time to first wish, whether the person opens an entrusted wish, whether they find their own wishes again, whether they understand lineage focus, and whether they distinguish ENCOURAGE / HELP / CONNECT without coaching.
