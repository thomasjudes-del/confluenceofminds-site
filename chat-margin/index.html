ChatMargin

A workspace that lives next to ChatGPT. The useful layer around a conversation stays visible: structured notes, reusable prompts, follow-ups, search and a full writing workspace.

Open ChatMargin ↗Add to Chrome

Why it belongs here.

Confluence of Minds is not only a shelf of finished worlds. It is also where tools emerge from the way those worlds are actually made.

ChatMargin began with a practical friction: long-running work inside ChatGPT generates useful fragments everywhere, while the structure around them disappears. ChatMargin keeps that structure in the margin, locally, without asking you to move the work into another system.

NotesPersistent, formatted notes attached to the work.
PromptsReusable instructions ready to insert where they matter.
DashboardFollow-ups and priorities that survive the conversation.
SearchFind across known chats, notes, sub-pages and prompts.
WorkspaceA full writing surface beside ChatGPT when the margin needs to expand.
ChatMargin is independent from OpenAI. Local-first. No ChatMargin account required.
