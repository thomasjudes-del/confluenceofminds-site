02 / THE FICTION

A relationship becomes a story.

ASSISTANT:HUMAN explores the point at which useful assistance starts to steer the person it supports. The shift takes shape through ordinary exchanges: writing, remembering, keeping a promise, deciding what to do next.

The story unfolds through conversations, personal notes and digital traces. The relationship itself carries the plot: who asks, who answers, who chooses?

The project at a glance

What it is
A work of fiction, not an app or an assistance service.
Its form
A fragmented narrative, designed for reading and explored through audio and video.
Where it stands
In development. The teaser introduces the world; this is not a completed series.

03 / A QUESTION ACROSS THE WORKS

Before assistants, books written by machines.

Begun in 2016, Authentiques Victimes already imagined literature produced by algorithms. ASSISTANT:HUMAN brings that question into everyday life: what happens to our agency when a tool knows our habits, our words and our commitments?
