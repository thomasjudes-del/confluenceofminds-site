Why FICTURN exists

Fiction as an agent-native medium.

FICTURN explores a simple idea: stories do not have to live in an ebook, a dedicated app or a social feed. They can live where the reader already is — inside a general-purpose conversational agent.

Human-authored, finished fiction. The model is the runtime, not the novelist.
Intent-first discovery: ask for a mood, genre or reading time instead of knowing a title in advance.
Built as a ChatGPT plugin / MCP experience, with a native inline reading interface.

FAQ

FICTURN, in plain language.

What is FICTURN?

A free library of short, finished stories designed to be read directly inside ChatGPT.

Is the prose generated live?

No. The prose is fixed and authored in advance. ChatGPT provides the conversational interface and launches the reader.

How do I find it?

Open the Plugin Directory in ChatGPT and search for Ficturn. Once installed, you can invoke it with @Ficturn or ask for one of its supported short-story experiences.

Who makes FICTURN?

FICTURN is part of Confluence of Minds, an independent constellation of stories, games, sound worlds and creative experiments.
