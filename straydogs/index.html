A few things to know.

Do I need a certain number of followers?

No. This is for independent creators with small, genuine audiences, including people still building theirs. The work, the care behind it and your willingness to engage matter more than a number.

Do I have to like or share everything?

No. Recommend what you genuinely enjoy and think your audience might enjoy too. There are no required likes, comments, shares or reciprocal promotion quotas.

Is the collective already running?

We are gathering a founding cohort. This page is an invitation to help shape the collective. Sending an introduction starts a conversation; it is not automatic membership or a promise of a match.

Do I need to use AI in my work?

No. Human-made, tool-assisted and hybrid practices are welcome. AI-assisted discovery may be explored later; there is no live matching tool on this page.

What am I committing to?

Start with a private introduction by email. Any collaboration, scope, credit, rights or payment would be agreed directly between the creators involved before work begins. There is no required posting schedule.

Will my application be public?

Your introduction is sent privately to Thomas at Confluence of Minds through your own email app. It is not published by this website. Only include information you want to share. You can use the same email address to ask about your introduction or withdraw your interest.
